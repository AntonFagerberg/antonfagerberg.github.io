Another step on my "[bespoke software](/ai-and-bespoke-software/)" journey. There's a lot of content that I enjoy on YouTube but I find it very difficult to have a healthy relationship with the normal YouTube app and website. It works for a while but eventually I drift away in weird directions because of the algorithm and start watching way too much YouTube shorts out of convenience.

Having a dedicated desktop app that only do the very basics of what I want is golden for me. This little app lets me subscribe to channels (via the official RSS feed) and stream it (using [mpv](https://mpv.io)) or save it for later (with [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)).
