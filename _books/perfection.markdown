This book was amazing but also painful to read at the same time. On a high level it's a fictional story but it puts a finger on a lot of thing that's wrong with my generation (millennials) and the time of our young adult lives.

It's about perfection, the searching for authenticity, having real life collapse in on us while still portraying a facade on social media. It's about everyone becoming interested in things like fermentation at the same time "randomly". It's cynical but it's the best thing I've read all year. Loved it!
