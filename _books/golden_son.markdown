This is the follow up book to Red Rising and it's a perfect sequel. It expands the story with more "world building" which I really enjoyed while remaining very action packed. This could easily be the next blockbuster movie or tv-show.

It's very easy to read, always some twist to keep you on your toes and always something new happening. I get that it might not be for everyone or a literary work of art, but if you want an action loaded sci-fi page-turner then this is a perfect fit. It got me out of a reading slump and I will continue with the series.
