I picked this up at the sci-fi section but it felt more like fantasy or even fairy tale-ish. There's a nugget of something brilliant here but it's not fully realized and at times it doesn't really make sense. There are some really good sections but there are also some bad, or perhaps not bad but boring and non-engaging, flailing sections.

In the end it just left me feeling "meh". It was a while since I read it and looking back I have some fond memories of the good parts but it's not a book I would recommend.
