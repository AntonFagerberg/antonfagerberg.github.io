On one hand this is a well written book about Koreans while their country is occupied by Japan, and later as immigrants in Japan as Korea got divided into south and north. The historical parts are interesting and the characters feel alive.

On the other hand, this book is about a woman and her parents and her in-laws and her children and her grandchildren and their spouses and so on and they all have some kind of a terrible misfortune. One traumatic life after the other. All things miserable covered.
