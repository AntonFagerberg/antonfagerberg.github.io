This is a collection of various autobiographic stories by Charles Bukowski on the topic of drinking and alcohol (plus some interviews). I'm torn when it comes to this book. Bukowski is an amazing writer and storyteller. Many of the stories are highly entertaining but also sad and upsetting (when you stop laughing). It's a lot of stories about an alcoholic doing dumb stuff, wasting his life and making other people miserable or generally misbehaving in various ways.

Bukowski is gifted though so it can still be worth a read.
