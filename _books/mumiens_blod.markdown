This is a Swedish graphic novel that I got from my dad many years ago. It's always fun to read something which takes place in Sweden even though it's mostly in the background. The story is an occult or super-natural mystery / detective-ish story. 

It's a little gory but it can probably be enjoyed by the older children. Not super deep but enough to keep me interested. Good pacing, not too much text. A perfect read for a vacation day (perhaps in the shade on a hot summers day like I did). You can easily finish it in one session.
