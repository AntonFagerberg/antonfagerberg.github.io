This must be one of the most original books I've read. I'm not sure how to describe it, something like a sci-fi deeply rooted in religion (christianity / judaism) done by someone who probably knows a thing or two about programming. It's very weird but also very good, and clever. I learned a lot of strange things about religion.

It can be tedious at times and it took me a while to finish but it's definitely worth a read, it's something very different. Check it out, not sure how to sell it in properly though...

[Available online!](https://unsongbook.com)
